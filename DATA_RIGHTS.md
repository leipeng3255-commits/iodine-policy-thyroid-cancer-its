## Included aggregate inputs and outputs

The repository contains no individual-level or restricted-access data and no
third-party raw workbooks, PDFs, HTML captures, or JSON downloads. Included CSV
files contain public aggregate rates/counts, model results, diagnostics, or
author-created figure/table source data.

The governing source terms are retained as follows:

| File group | Principal source | Governing reuse boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Australia incidence | Australian Institute of Health and Welfare, Cancer data in Australia | Public-download material; AIHW states that most released information is available under CC BY 4.0. Attribution and the source URL are retained. |
| New Zealand incidence | Health New Zealand / Te Whatu Ora cancer web tool | The source tool states CC BY 4.0. Attribution, version, and source URL are retained. |
| Croatia incidence, 1988–2017 | IARC Cancer Over Time / CI5plus | IARC permits free use subject to non-sale/non-commercial or promotional use and appropriate acknowledgment. These data are not sublicensed under MIT or CC BY. |
| Croatia incidence, 2018–2023 | Croatian Institute of Public Health cancer-registry publications | Only extracted aggregate factual values are included; official publication attribution and URLs are retained. No licence broader than the source terms is asserted. |
| Mortality | WHO Mortality Database / IARC presentation | Aggregate derived values are included with WHO/IARC attribution and source terms. No WHO or IARC endorsement is implied. |
| Histology | IARC CI5plus detailed data | IARC non-sale/non-commercial and acknowledgment conditions remain attached; no broader sublicence is asserted. |
| Policy and UIC context | FSANZ, WHO micronutrient sources, New Zealand health sources, Croatian official gazette and cited literature | Only small author-curated aggregate/context values are included. Each underlying source retains its own terms. |
| Model, diagnostic, table and figure outputs | Authors, derived from the source groups above | Available for scholarly verification, subject to the upstream data terms and attribution chain. The MIT License does not apply to these CSVs. |

Full source records, versions, access dates, URLs, and raw-source SHA-256 values
are provided in `metadata/`. Users seeking the original data must obtain them
from the official providers and comply with the providers' current terms.

World Health Organization and IARC names and marks must not be used to imply
endorsement. No logos or emblems are included in this repository.
